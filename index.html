REEFLY

Smart Monitoring for Healthier Coral Reefs

REEFLY is an underwater monitoring system designed to observe coral reef health, detect changes and provide useful information to help protect marine ecosystems.

Discover REEFLY

The Problem

🌊 Pollution

Plastic, chemicals and other pollutants can damage coral reef ecosystems.

🌡️ Climate Change

Rising ocean temperatures can cause coral bleaching and weaken reef ecosystems.

🚤 Tourism

Increasing human activity can put additional pressure on fragile coral reefs.

🐠 Ecosystem Loss

Damaged coral reefs can affect fish, marine life and coastal communities.

How REEFLY Works

1. Underwater Monitoring

REEFLY uses underwater monitoring technology to observe coral reef environments.

2. Collect Reef Information

The system can collect visual and environmental information about the reef.

3. Detect Changes

Changes in reef conditions can be identified by analysing the collected information.

4. Take Action

The information can help researchers and conservation teams understand where protection or restoration is needed.

Our Technology

📷 Underwater Cameras: Capture images and video of coral reef conditions.

📊 Data Monitoring: Helps track changes in reef health over time.

🤖 Smart Analysis: Technology can assist in identifying important changes in underwater environments.

🌊 Continuous Observation: Regular monitoring can provide a clearer picture of how reefs are changing.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make coral reef monitoring smarter, faster and more accessible. By understanding reef health earlier, we can support better decisions to protect marine ecosystems for the future.
