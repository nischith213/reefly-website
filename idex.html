REEFLY is a smart underwater monitoring concept designed to observe coral reef conditions, identify visible changes and support better decisions for marine ecosystem protection.

📷 Our Technology

Underwater cameras act as the eyes of REEFLY beneath the ocean surface. They capture visual information that can help us understand changing reef conditions.

📷

Capture

Underwater cameras capture images and videos of monitored coral reef areas.

🔍

Observe

Footage can be reviewed for visible coral damage, pollution and other changes.

📊

Track

Observations from different periods can be compared to track changes over time.

📊 Reef Monitoring

REEFLY focuses on visible indicators that can help teams understand reef conditions.

🪸 Coral Condition

Observe healthy, stressed or visibly damaged areas.

🗑️ Visible Pollution

Record visible waste and pollution around monitored reef areas.

🐠 Marine Life

Observe marine life around the monitored reef.

⚠️ Physical Damage

Identify visible disturbances that may require attention.

📈 Changes Over Time

Compare observations from different monitoring periods.

Our mission is to combine underwater monitoring, useful data and community action to support healthier reefs.
